Stuck on a step? Found a broken link? Tell us. Your note goes to a human, and it makes the site better for the next reader.

How to reach us

1. Email (fastest)

Write to contact@androidscroll.com about anything: a correction, a broken link, a guide request, a business inquiry.

To get a fast, correct answer:

  • Say which page you mean, and paste its link.
  • If a step failed on your phone, name your phone model and Android version. On most phones: open Settings, tap About phone.
  • We answer every real message within 1 to 2 business days.

2. Social media

For a quick, casual word, message us here:

Facebook: Like our page on Facebook

X (Twitter): Follow us @AndroidScroll

What to write about

  • Corrections: a step that fails, a screenshot that looks old, a menu path that moved.
  • Broken things: dead links, pages that load wrong, search that misses.
  • New guides: the Android problem you want us to test next.
  • Business: partnerships and press.

Tip: if your question is about one guide, comment on that page instead. The answer then helps every reader, not just you.
