Affiliate links

Some links on AndroidScroll are affiliate links to shops such as Amazon, Jumia, and Konga. If you tap one and buy, the shop pays us a small fee from its own pocket.

What this means for you:

  • Same price. You pay no more through our link than by walking into the shop yourself.
  • Your data stays yours. We never see your name, address, or card. We only learn that a buy happened, and when.
  • Fees never pick winners. A fee cannot turn a bad product into a recommendation. When we get a pick wrong, the page will say so.

How to support us

Running this site costs time and money: testing, writing, editing, hosting. If our guides helped you, here is how to give back:

  • Shop through our links when you already plan to buy. It costs you nothing.
  • Leave a comment with what worked on your phone. Your words teach the next reader.
  • Share the guide that fixed your problem, anywhere friends ask for help.

Advice, not gospel

We test every step, but phones differ by brand, system version, and carrier. Back up what matters before you tap. If a step fails on your phone, tell us through the Contact page and we will fix the page.
