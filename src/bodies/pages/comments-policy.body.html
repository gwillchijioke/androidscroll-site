Talk to us. Ask questions, report a step that failed, share what worked on your phone. These rules keep the talk useful for the next reader too.

By posting a comment, you agree to the rules below.

1. Who you are

  • Use a real name or one steady nickname. Names stuffed with keywords (such as a company name in the name box) read as spam and will be treated as spam.
  • Give a working email. It never shows in public. We use it only to reach you about your comment.
  • Write in English so the whole room can follow.
  • No photo needed. Every reader shows as a plain monogram next to their name. We pull no profile photos from outside services.

2. How to act

  • Stay kind. This is a family site. Rude or foul words go out.
  • Fault the point, not the person. Question the guide all you like. Leave the writer and other readers alone.
  • Add something. "Step 3 failed on my phone, here is what I saw" beats "nice post". Empty or off-track notes may be cut.

3. Links and spam

  • Two links at most, and only when they help. More than two waits in line for a human check.
  • No signature links. Your own site does not ride along with your name.
  • Spam earns a ban. Spam is deleted on sight. Repeat offenders have that IP address, domain, and email blocked.

4. Review and removal

  • Every comment waits for a human. Filters catch most spam first, then we read the rest. Nothing shows until we pass it. Most pass within one business day.
  • We may edit, move, or cut any comment that breaks these rules, and block repeat breakers.
  • Your words stay yours. By posting, you let us show them on this site, and you hold us free of blame for what you wrote.
  • Want out? Write to contact@androidscroll.com and we will wipe your comment and its data for good.

Common questions

  • Where is my comment? In the review line. If a full business day passes and you broke no rule, reach us through our Contact page.

Thank you for making this room worth reading.
