At AndroidScroll, we believe that interaction is what makes our community great. We encourage you to share your thoughts, ask questions, and engage with our tutorials.

​To maintain a high-quality resource and protect our readers from spam, we have established the following comment policy. By commenting on this site, you agree to these terms.

1. Identification & Privacy

  • Comment Name: Please use your real name or a consistent nickname. We do not allow “keyword stuffing” (e.g., using “Best Web Design Company” as your name). These comments will be flagged as spam.
  • Valid Email: A valid email address is required. Your email will never be published or shared; it is strictly used for verification.
  • Language: To ensure our community can understand and benefit from the conversation, please leave your comments in English.

2. Community Standards

  • Keep it Friendly: AndroidScroll is a family-friendly site. We do not tolerate offensive, rude, or inappropriate language.
  • ​No Personal Attacks: You are welcome to question or argue the content of an article, but please do not attack the author or other commenters.
  • ​Add Value: We love comments that move the conversation forward. Comments that are off-topic or “kill” the discussion may be edited or removed.

3. Links & Spam Prevention

  • Limit Links: You may include up to two links if they are relevant resources. Comments with more than two links will be held for manual review.
  • ​No Backlinks: Please do not sign your comments with a link to your website. These will be removed to keep the focus on the discussion.
  • ​Zero Tolerance for Spam: All spam comments will be deleted. Continuous abuse of these policies will result in a permanent ban of the IP address, domain, and email used.

4. Rights & Liability

  • Moderation: We reserve the right to edit, delete, move, or mark any comment as spam. We also reserve the right to block access to anyone who repeatedly violates these rules.
  • ​Liability: All comments are the responsibility of the individual commenter. By submitting a comment, you agree that the content is your own and you agree to hold AndroidScroll and its representatives harmless from any liability.

Common Questions

  • Where is my comment? If your comment doesn’t appear within one business day and you haven’t violated any policies, please reach out via our Contact Page.
  • How do I set my Profile Picture? This site uses WordPress. To show a display picture (avatar) next to your comment here and on other blogs, create a free account at Gravatar.
